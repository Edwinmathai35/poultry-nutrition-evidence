# Four-day publication sequence

| Day | Question and evidence | Core figures | Message boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Side Quest 11 | What do poultry feed records actually measure? Explain USDA price ratio, 200 complete months, stationarity and seasonality. | 01, 02, 03 | Economic purchasing power, not farm FCR or African feed costs. |
| Side Quest 12 | Does a more complex forecast improve decisions? Show fixed candidate comparison, development/holdout separation, prediction intervals and residual limitations. | 04, 05, 06 | The selected models did not beat naive on holdout MAE. Preserve that result. |
| Side Quest 13 | Can lower-protein diets maintain performance? Introduce trial, randomization, DAG, pen unit, FCR and ADG effects. | 11, 07, 08 | No significant FCR contrasts is not equivalence; low-protein soybean-free whole ration reduced ADG. |
| Side Quest 14 | What makes a result safe to use? Link phase analysis, sensitivity, source reconciliation and tested governance controls to local data-system requirements. | 09, 10, 12 | Internal checks are not independent certification; local and operational validation remain necessary. |

Each carousel should use one large authentic R chart per results slide and one short interpretation. Light ivory, blue and lavender; no green. No decorative slide IDs, invented numbers or AI-redrawn charts. Provide study/data DOI and repository link only when a repository actually exists. Captions should be 2,925-2,975 characters when drafted, with programmatic counts. Do not publish the forecast ratio as feed cost per bird. State the selected data dates, units, experimental unit, confidence level and material limitations.

Potential × Management × System = Performance is a conceptual campaign framework, not a fitted mathematical identity in these datasets.
